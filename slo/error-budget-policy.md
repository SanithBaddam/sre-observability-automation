# Error Budget Policy

The error budget converts the SLO into an operational decision tool.

## Policy

- **Healthy budget:** normal feature delivery continues.
- **Moderate burn:** prioritize reliability work alongside planned delivery.
- **Fast burn:** freeze risky releases and page the owning team.
- **Budget exhausted:** reliability work takes priority until the service returns to a sustainable operating state.

## Review cadence

Teams should review:
- current SLO attainment
- 1h / 6h / 24h burn rates
- major incident contribution
- recurring failure modes
- planned changes that could affect reliability

Error budgets should guide decisions rather than become a punitive performance metric.
