# SEV-1 Incident Runbook

## First 10 minutes
1. Confirm customer impact and establish incident command.
2. Identify the failing service boundary and recent changes.
3. Stabilize the system before pursuing root cause.
4. Assign communications and investigation roles.
5. Record a timeline as actions occur.

## Mitigation priority
Prefer reversible actions: rollback, traffic shift, feature disablement, capacity increase, or dependency isolation.

## Exit criteria
Customer impact has stopped, critical indicators are recovering, and the system is stable enough to move from mitigation to investigation.

## Post-incident
Produce a blameless timeline, contributing factors, detection gaps, and owners/dates for corrective actions.
